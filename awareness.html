Security Awareness

Practical guidance to reduce human-risk in your organisation

🔑 Use Strong Passwords

Aim for 12+ characters mixing letters, numbers, and symbols. Better yet — use a passphrase like "coffee-lamp-orbit-seven".

🛡️ Enable MFA

Multi-factor authentication blocks the vast majority of account takeover attempts, even when the password is leaked.

🎣 Spot Phishing

Check the sender domain, hover over links before clicking, and be wary of urgency ("act now", "account suspended").

🔄 Update Regularly

Patches close known vulnerabilities. Enable automatic updates on your OS, browser, and applications.

💾 Back Up Data

Follow the 3-2-1 rule: 3 copies, on 2 different media, with 1 stored off-site. Ransomware becomes far less scary.

🧠 Think Before You Click

Slow down. Attackers rely on rushed decisions. If something feels off, verify through a separate channel.
