Security Awareness

Short, practical guidance. Most real-world breaches start with one of these five gaps.

Passwords

Use a passphrase of at least 12 characters. Never reuse a password across services. A password manager is the easiest way to do this properly.

Multi-Factor Authentication

Enable MFA on every account that supports it, especially email. This alone blocks the vast majority of account takeover attempts.

Spotting Phishing

Check the actual sender domain, not the display name. Hover over links before clicking. Treat urgency as a red flag, not a reason to hurry.

Updates

Keep your operating system, browser, and applications up to date. Patches close the vulnerabilities attackers actually use.

Backups

Follow the 3-2-1 rule: three copies, two media, one off-site. This turns ransomware from a disaster into an inconvenience.

Reporting

If something feels wrong, report it. A five-minute report is cheaper than a five-day incident. Security teams would rather get ten false alarms than miss one real attack.

CyberShield is an educational simulation. No real systems are monitored.
