Glossary

Terms I keep coming back to while studying red team work.

Red Team

A group that emulates real attackers to test an organisation's detection and response capability. Goal is to improve defence, not to cause damage.

Blue Team

The defensive side: monitoring, detection, incident response, hardening. Red and blue are complementary, not opposites.

Foothold

The first reliable piece of access an attacker has on a target system. Everything else builds from here.

Lateral Movement

Moving from one compromised host to another inside the same network, usually to reach more valuable systems.

C2 (Command and Control)

The channel an attacker uses to send instructions to a compromised host and receive results back.

Payload

The code that actually does the work once it runs — opening a shell, exfiltrating a file, installing persistence.

OPSEC

Operational Security. In red team terms: not getting caught, not leaving obvious traces, not burning your access.

MITRE ATT&CK

Public framework of real-world adversary tactics and techniques. Used on both sides — red teams structure operations by it, blue teams map detections to it.
